School life

During 2024, I entered Senior High School at Gothong National High School. During my time as a TVL student, I learned many new things and gained valuable knowledge and skills. I especially learned a lot from our major subject, Electrical Installation and Maintenance (EIM). Through EIM, I developed practical skills and gained a better understanding of electrical work, safety procedures, and the proper use of tools and equipment. Along the way also made a lot of friends that help me in my journey in senior high.
